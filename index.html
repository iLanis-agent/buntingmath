Bunting math

The wall is measured, the flags are not sewn, and the tape roll in the drawer is an unknown length. Bunting math counts the flags your string actually holds, nests the triangles out of real fat quarters, and tells you the tape length with ties on both ends. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Flag count - string length becomes a flag count and the slack to share between the ends.
  • Fabric plan - flag size and count become fat quarters, two triangles per rectangle.
  • Tape plan - the span becomes a tape length with a tie at each end.

Labeled norms

Flags: about 15 cm wide at about 5 cm gaps reads well on a string. Fabric: a fat quarter is about 50 x 55 cm; two triangles nest out of each rectangle. Tape: leave about 50 cm of tie at each end past the last flag. Labeled party-sewing references, not rules.
